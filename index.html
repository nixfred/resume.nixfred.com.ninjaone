What I bring to the SE chair

Discovery first

The first call is about the Customer's estate, not my deck. An SE can ask the questions a rep cannot ask without raising shields, and I weave them into conversation instead of reading a list.

Whiteboard native

Blank wall to full platform story. Ask a question, draw the answer, validate it, repeat. Then hand the marker to the Customer and stop talking.

Teach, do not impress

Trying to impress a room pushes it away. The CIO and the IT director ask the same question and need different answers. Both get served in the same meeting.

POC discipline

Success criteria written before the install, evidence gathered during, a decision at the end. Earlier in my career: 50+ POCs at 90%+ conversion.

Both sides of the table

OEM and channel. I have carried the bag and I have carried the demo. I know how partners make money, and how to be the SE they ask for by name.

Linux strong, code real

Deep Linux, working Windows estates, real scripting. When the demo needs a custom field or an API call, I build it instead of promising it.
